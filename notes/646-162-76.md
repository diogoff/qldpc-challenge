# [[646,162,76]] cyclic generalized-bicycle code on Z_323

## Direction & hypothesis

Target the unrestricted weight-9plus frontier, where high-rate
generalized-bicycle entries near n=682 have k around 170 and witnessed d in
the 70s. The factorization structure of x^323-1 offers shared-divisor degrees
near 80 while keeping n=646, so sparse words from a shared ideal could give a
lower-blocklength, high-rate alternative. The goal was a board-relative Pareto
improvement, not a literature-novelty claim.

## What was searched

Factored x^323-1 and examined divisor ideals with target degree 80. Prange
sampling searched 100 ideals for sparse words in weight bands 14-16, using
10,000 trials per ideal; five ideals were fertile. Pairing their words
produced 390 cyclic-GB candidates, screened with 4,000 fast RIS trials per
candidate. The shortlist was built at n=646, k=162, max check weight 32. The
winning pair's 300,000-trial CUDA recovery read X=95 and Z=88; two fresh
2,000,000-trial-per-side CUDA rungs then read X=76/Z=79 and X=76/Z=78.

## Dead ends

- At m=301, no degree-85 ideal was fertile in 100 sampled ideals; at m=329, no
  degree-92 ideal was fertile in the same budget.
- At m=323, two nearby high-screen pairs collapsed under deeper GPU searches:
  one was reduced to d <= 84, and another to d <= 30. Their lower-weight operators
  were retained in the staged candidate records.
- A separate 25,000-proposal bivariate-bicycle screen within n<=700 produced
  no validated improvement; a [[540,8,40]] lead fell to d<=30 in a
  2,000,000-trial matched-depth audit against the [[540,8,32]] peer.

b=[13,51,55,67,118,135,146,152,169,186,207,220,237,254,264,305]. Form

has n=646, k=162, and maximum check weight 32.
