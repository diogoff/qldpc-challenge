# [[646,162,48]] cyclic generalized-bicycle code on Z_323

## Direction & hypothesis

Target the unrestricted weight-9plus frontier with a high-rate cyclic generalized-bicycle construction below n=700. The factorization of x^323-1 permits a shared divisor of degree 80, giving k=162 at n=646. Sparse words from that ideal offered a route to a shorter high-rate code than the existing [[674,168,76]] point, though with a potentially lower distance.

## What was searched

Factored x^323-1 and sampled 100 degree-80 divisor ideals. Prange sampling searched for words in weight bands 14-16 using 10,000 trials per ideal; five ideals were fertile. Pairing their words produced 390 cyclic-GB candidates, screened with 4,000 fast RIS trials each. The selected pair initially screened at d<=85. CUDA recovery at 300,000 trials per side found X=95 and Z=88; two fresh 2,000,000-trial-per-side runs found X=76/Z=79 and X=76/Z=78.

The 400,000-trial structural pass produced the explicit weight-48 X-logical carried in the code JSON; the verifier confirms it commutes with the opposite checks and is not an X stabilizer. A later independent 2,000,000-trial-per-side CUDA run (seed 1317976217) read X=78 and Z=77 and did not reproduce the lighter X witness. This is a search miss, not a contradiction of the checkable witness. Two subsequent trusted candidate-gate seeds, 1317976216 and 20260946, accepted the corrected d<=48 claim.

## Dead ends

- At m=301, none of 100 sampled degree-85 ideals was fertile; at m=329, none of 100 sampled degree-92 ideals was fertile.
- Other high-screen m=323 pairs collapsed under deeper checks, including candidates corrected to d<=84 and d<=30.
- A separate 25,000-proposal bivariate-bicycle screen within n<=700 produced no validated improvement. Its [[540,8,40]] lead fell to d<=30 in a 2,000,000-trial matched-depth audit against [[540,8,32]].
- The original [[646,162,76]] claim was refuted by the CI circulant-GB structural pass and is replaced by this d<=48 record.

## Tools

Form H_X=[circ(a)|circ(b)] and H_Z=[circ(b)^T|circ(a)^T]. This gives n=646 and k=162. The submission carries an X witness of weight 48 and a Z witness of weight 77.
